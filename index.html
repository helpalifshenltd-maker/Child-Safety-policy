Child Safety Standards

Alif Blood Bank

Developed by Alif sheen group ltd

1. Our Commitment to Child Safety

Alif Blood Bank, operated by Alif sheen group ltd, is committed to protecting children and maintaining a safe digital environment. We have a zero-tolerance policy toward Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE).

2. Strict Prohibition of CSAE

We strictly prohibit the following activities:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).
  • Sexual exploitation or abuse of minors.
  • Child grooming or inappropriate sexual communication with children.
  • Sharing, uploading, distributing, or promoting content that exploits children.
  • Any activity that puts children at risk of abuse or exploitation.

3. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users can report suspicious activities, inappropriate content, or child safety concerns through our official support contact. All reports will be reviewed and appropriate action will be taken.

4. Enforcement and Content Removal

Alif Blood Bank will take appropriate action against policy violations, including:

  • Removing prohibited content.
  • Suspending or terminating violating user accounts.
  • Investigating reported child safety concerns.
  • Reporting confirmed CSAM cases to appropriate authorities when legally required.

5. Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)

We do not allow the creation, sharing, storage, or distribution of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) through our platform. We will take appropriate action when such material is identified or reported.

6. Compliance with Child Safety Laws

Alif sheen group ltd is committed to complying with all applicable child protection laws and regulations. We cooperate with relevant authorities when required by law.
